Procedural Terrain Generation API (Unity)

RoleProject Owner, Design Lead
FocusSystem Design · Mechanics Design · Player Experience

Overview

Developed a procedural terrain generation system in Unity designed to create dynamic and replayable environments. The goal was to build a flexible tool capable of generating varied terrain efficiently and consistently.

Challenges

The initial system suffered from performance issues and repetitive output. Terrain generation was computationally heavy, causing slowdowns, and generated environments often lacked visual and structural variety.

Solution

Optimized generation logic by reducing unnecessary calculations and improving how terrain data was stored and reused. Variation rules and parameter tuning helped produce more natural and diverse environments across runs.

Outcome

The final system delivered improved performance and significantly more environmental variety. It became a stable, scalable procedural tool suitable for larger and more complex projects.

Mechanics + Theme Emphasis

RoleProject Owner, Systems Designer, Developer
FocusSystems Design · Theme Integration · Playability

Overview

Designed and developed a fully playable 100-card Commander deck for Magic: The Gathering, centered around three original custom commanders. The goal was to create a cohesive system combining unique mechanics, strong thematic identity, and practical playability within the Commander format.

Challenges

Balancing multiple custom commanders created complexity. Each character had distinct mechanics, and the deck needed to work together without overwhelming players or creating conflicting strategies. Accessibility and overall balance were additional constraints.

Solution

The design was unified through a shared mechanic—harmony counters—which connected each commander’s playstyle. Interactions were simplified for readability, emphasizing token generation, combat synergy, and resilience. Commonly available cards were prioritized, and the deck was formatted for Moxfield to support testing.

Outcome

The result was a functional Commander deck with a clear identity and smooth gameplay flow. Its mechanics and card choices blended creative theme with practical play.

Narrative Design: Tabletop Campaign

RoleProject Owner, Design Lead
FocusNarrative Design · Player Choice · System Structure

Overview

Created an original tabletop campaign inspired by The Dark Tower, focused on player-driven storytelling and meaningful choices. The goal was to build a system where decisions had clear and lasting impacts on the world and narrative.

Challenges

Some mechanics and narrative paths were initially too complex, making it difficult for players to understand how their decisions influenced the game. This slowed gameplay and reduced engagement.

Solution

Simplified core systems and restructured how information was presented. Clearer decision feedback through in-world consequences made outcomes easier to recognize. Playtesting helped identify confusion points and refine the experience.

Outcome

The final version delivered a smoother gameplay experience. Players could better understand how their actions shaped the narrative world and make choices with greater confidence.

Exit Alone

RoleGame Designer · Team Project
FocusCompetitive Cooperation · Resource Management · Player Interaction

Overview

Exit Alone takes place in a science facility where experiments have taken over and the facility is nearing lockdown. Players work together to defeat threats and search for three security keys. Once a player has the keys, they must escape while the others can sabotage their attempt and take the keys for themselves.

Design Goal

Create tension between temporary cooperation and individual victory, using shared threats and scarce objectives to encourage shifting alliances.

Outcome

A competitive survival board-game concept built around exploration, tactical decisions, and a race to escape.
